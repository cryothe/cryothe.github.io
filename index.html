Samuel Pham

A few unsolicited facts about a guy named Samuel Pham — computer science student, late-night debugger, and occasional builder of worlds that only exist on a screen.

  • Samuel Pham can tell you the difference between a semicolon and a colon, but still pauses at the grocery self-checkout.
  • He once named a variable finalFinal2 and still thinks about it at night.
  • Samuel believes a good playlist can fix a broken build. The data does not support this. He keeps trying anyway.
  • He prefers notebooks with too many pages and terminals with too many tabs.
  • If you ask Samuel what he does for fun, he will mention 3D games, then quietly add that he also enjoys walking without a destination.
  • He has strong opinions about coffee temperature and weak opinions about which JS framework will still exist in five years.
  • Samuel Pham is the kind of person who reads error messages out loud, as if the compiler might feel guilty.
  • He keeps a running list of side projects that starts with “portfolio website” and somehow always includes a 3D RPG.
