Projects

A short look at things Samuel has been building — one for the web, one for a world with a camera, a map, and a sword.

Personal Portfolio

HTML · CSS · multi-page layout

A clean personal site for Samuel Pham, designed as a small gallery of pages rather than a single long scroll. The portfolio focuses on typography, a restrained beige palette, and simple page-to-page transitions so the work can speak without extra chrome. It is both the project and the place the other projects live.

3D RPG Game

3D · game design · exploration

A third-person RPG built around wandering, combat, and a world that feels a little larger than the screen. Players move through a 3D environment, pick up quests, and fight through encounters while the camera, lighting, and level layout do as much storytelling as the dialogue. The project is Samuel’s playground for game systems: character movement, inventories, and the small details that make a place feel worth exploring.
